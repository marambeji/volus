# Calendars and Team Availability

## "Who's Out" (dashboard widget)

The employee dashboard shows a quick "Who's Out" panel with **Today** and **Tomorrow** tabs,
listing every employee who is on approved or pending leave that day, with a visual indicator
of whether they're out for a full day or a half day.

## Full Calendar

A dedicated calendar page lets anyone browse absences by day, week, month, or year. It has
three scopes:

- **My Circle / Team** (the default) — just the people directly relevant to you.
- **All Employees** — the whole company.
- **Custom Filter** — hand-pick a specific set of employees to focus on.

Clicking any day opens a list of everyone who's out that specific day, across whichever scope
is selected.

## Team Availability (managers)

From the Approval Dashboard, a manager can open a **Team Schedule Overview**: a calendar of
the whole team's approved and pending leave over a date range they pick (the last/next 7, 14,
or 30 days, the current month, or a fully custom range). This is the tool managers use to
check "is anyone else already off around this time" before approving a request. The same
overview is also available scoped to just the people affected by one specific request, right
from that request's row on the Approval Dashboard.

## Company Directory and Org Chart

The **People** page is a searchable, filterable directory of every active employee — name,
job title, department, country, manager, and contact details — with a toggle between a plain
card list and an **interactive organization chart** view. Clicking an employee in the org
chart re-centers the chart around them, making it easy to explore reporting lines
department-by-department or manager-by-manager.
