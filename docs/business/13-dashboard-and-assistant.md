# Employee Dashboard and AI Assistant

## The dashboard

When an employee or manager logs in, their landing page is a dashboard made of a few
independent widgets:

- A welcome banner showing a greeting, whether they're on leave today, and how many days
  they have left, with a one-click "Request Time Off" button.
- A stats bar: available leave, pending days, days approved so far this year, and how many
  teammates are out today.
- "Who's Out" (see [Calendars and Team Availability](07-calendars-and-team-availability.md)).
- A card showing their most recent leave request and its approval progress.
- Upcoming public holidays (see [Public Holidays](08-public-holidays.md)).
- A **Company Links** widget — a searchable, categorized list of internal company links (for
  example, IT support, payroll, internal tools), maintained as a fixed reference list rather
  than something HR edits from the portal today.

## The AI HR Assistant

A chat assistant is available everywhere in the employee/manager portal (a floating chat
button). Employees can ask it plain-language questions about company policy — leave policy,
the employee handbook, the referral program, or medical insurance — and it answers grounded
in the actual text of those documents, rather than guessing. It's meant as a first stop for
"what does our policy say about X" questions, not a replacement for asking HR directly on
anything that needs a real decision (like an exception to policy).
