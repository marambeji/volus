# Audit Log

Every meaningful change made in the system is permanently recorded: who did it, what kind of
action it was, when it happened, and — where relevant — what the values were before and
after. This includes things like: an employee being created, updated, or removed; a leave
request being submitted, approved, rejected, cancelled, or deleted by HR; an individual
approval step being approved, rejected, or skipped; a balance being deducted, reversed, or
manually adjusted; and approval workflows being created, updated, or deleted.

## Two ways to see it

- **Audit Log page (HR only)** — the full, company-wide, searchable, filterable history of
  everything that's happened, with a filter by the type of thing changed and the type of
  action taken.
- **History on a specific record** — from many of the HR Admin screens (Employees, Leave
  Requests, Balances, Policies, Approval Levels), a "History" button opens just the changes
  relevant to that one record — for example, the full change history of one specific employee
  or one specific leave request — without having to search through the global log.

## Why it matters

The audit log is the definitive answer to "who changed this and why" — every entry that
requires a reason (rejections, HR deletions, manual balance adjustments) has that reason
permanently attached to its audit entry, so HR always has a record to fall back on for
disputes or reviews.
